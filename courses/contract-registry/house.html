HOW TO ADD ITEMS WITHOUT CODING

Click the red "＋ Add Item" button in the top bar at any time. Choose any of the 7 rooms, pick Daraz Drawer, Vault Secret, or Flashcard, and click Spawn in 3D House Now. Your 3D house immediately updates its drawer counts and spawns physical 3D artifacts in Room 7!

HOW TO PLUG INTO `registry.sealed.json`

In your repo (`courses/contract-registry/js/app.js`), route #/house mounts this 3D view and passes the decrypted data object from boot.js into window.mountRegistryHouse(data). The house automatically populates its Daraz drawers from data.contract, data.cases, and data.letters!
